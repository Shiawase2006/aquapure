Recommended Chlorine-Free Purification Methods

Chlorine-Free Purification Methods

Interactive animations demonstrating how each purification method works

SODIS (Solar Disinfection)

Uses solar UV radiation and heat to inactivate pathogens in PET bottles exposed to sunlight for 6+ hours.

6–48 hours exposure
Effectiveness: High for Bacteria/Viruses

Activated Carbon Filtration

Removes organic compounds, chlorine, and improves taste/odor through adsorption on carbon surface.

Removes chemicals & odors
Effectiveness: Chemicals & Taste

Reverse Osmosis

Semi-permeable membrane removes dissolved solids, heavy metals, and microorganisms under pressure.

Removes 95–99% contaminants
Effectiveness: Comprehensive

Boiling

Heating water to 100°C for 1–3 minutes kills pathogens, viruses, and bacteria effectively.

100°C for 1–3 minutes
Effectiveness: Pathogen Elimination

UV Radiation

Ultraviolet light disrupts DNA of microorganisms, preventing reproduction without chemicals.

Instant disinfection
Effectiveness: Microorganism Control

Combination Systems

Integrated multi-stage systems combining multiple methods for comprehensive water treatment.

Multi-stage purification
Effectiveness: Maximum Protection
